`nessai` is a nested sampling package that incorporates normalizing flows.

I am the lead developer of nessai, which I started during my PhD and have maintained
and developed since. It has since been used for a wide range of analyses, including
applications in LVK, LISA and ET analyses, as well as applications in multimessenger
astronomy and experimental physics.
